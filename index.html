POLICY 01Education

Equal Opportunity School Funding Act

Expanding Access to Quality Public Education

We propose increasing federal Title I-A education funding by 10%, providing approximately $1.84 billion annually in additional resources to underfunded public schools. This investment will prioritize low-income and rural communities, supporting qualified teachers, tutoring programs, and essential academic materials. Schools receiving additional funds will establish three-year improvement plans and publicly report their progress in student achievement. By directing resources toward communities with greater educational needs, we aim to ensure that every student has a fair opportunity to succeed regardless of their family's financial background.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$1.84B
AT A GLANCE
  • 10% increase in Title I-A funding
  • Three-year school improvement plans
  • Priority for low-income and rural communities
POLICY 02Education

Teacher Excellence and Academic Improvement Act

Strengthening Education Through Better Teaching

We propose investing an additional $220 million annually in teacher development and academic improvement programs. Federal grants will support teacher mentoring, professional training, and evidence-based instructional practices, with participating states generally contributing matching funds. State and local governments will retain authority over curricula, while parents will receive greater transparency regarding school performance. This approach seeks to improve academic quality without imposing a centralized national curriculum.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$220M
AT A GLANCE
  • Teacher mentoring and professional training
  • State matching funds generally required
  • State and local control of curricula
POLICY 03Economy

Fair Competition and AI Accountability Act

Protecting Innovation Through Fair Competition

We support a competitive free-market economy while strengthening protections against monopolistic practices, worker exploitation, and unfair corporate behavior. Our proposal allocates an additional $150 million annually to the Federal Trade Commission and Department of Justice for stronger antitrust enforcement and consumer protection. Large commercial AI developers will face clearer disclosure requirements concerning copyrighted materials used in training, with appropriate safeguards for trade secrets. Smaller businesses will receive simplified compliance requirements to encourage entrepreneurship and technological innovation.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$150M
AT A GLANCE
  • FTC and DOJ competition enforcement
  • AI training-data transparency
  • Simplified requirements for small businesses
POLICY 04Economy

Fair Taxation and Responsible Investment Act

Modernizing Taxes Without Burdening the Middle Class

We propose a modest 2% additional tax on annual adjusted gross income exceeding $5 million for individuals or $10 million for married couples filing jointly. Our policy will also reform preferential tax treatment for certain investment-management compensation, including carried interest, while preserving incentives for entrepreneurship and investment. An additional $150 million annually will strengthen tax administration, improve compliance, and address complex high-income tax cases. Revenue from these reforms will help finance our public investments, with program expansion dependent on independent estimates confirming sufficient funding.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$150M
AT A GLANCE
  • 2% additional tax above $5M individual / $10M joint AGI
  • Carried-interest tax reform
  • Independent revenue estimates required
POLICY 05National Defense

Strong Defense, Responsible Engagement Act

Maintaining Military Strength Without Unnecessary Intervention

We believe that a strong military is essential to protecting national security, but military strength should not automatically lead to greater involvement in foreign conflicts. Our policy will maintain defense readiness while strengthening congressional oversight of overseas military operations and defense expenditures. The Department of Defense will be directed to identify up to $500 million in potential administrative efficiencies for internal reallocation, subject to independent verification. We will maintain important international alliances while prioritizing domestic security, diplomatic solutions, and responsible use of military resources.

POTENTIAL INTERNAL EFFICIENCIESUp to $500M
AT A GLANCE
  • Maintain defense readiness and alliances
  • Congressional oversight of overseas operations
  • Up to $500M in potential internal efficiencies
POLICY 06National Defense

Constitutional Emergency Powers Protection Act

Protecting Individual Liberty During National Emergencies

We believe that protecting national security must not come at the expense of fundamental constitutional rights. Our proposal requires extraordinary emergency restrictions on civil liberties to meet strict necessity and proportionality standards, with access to expedited judicial review for eligible legal challenges. Covered restrictions will generally expire after 30 days unless lawfully renewed, with additional congressional oversight for extended measures. An annual investment of $50 million will support legal review, reporting, and oversight to ensure that emergency powers remain accountable and temporary.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$50M
AT A GLANCE
  • Strict necessity and proportionality review
  • Generally 30-day expiration
  • Expedited court review and congressional oversight
POLICY 07Social Welfare

Working Families Healthcare Affordability Act

Making Essential Healthcare More Affordable

We believe that individuals and families should not be denied essential healthcare simply because they cannot afford insurance costs. Our proposal will provide up to $2,000 annually in additional health insurance assistance to as many as two million eligible individuals, focusing on qualifying households earning approximately 138%–250% of the federal poverty level. The program will supplement existing Affordable Care Act Marketplace assistance while preventing duplicate benefits and requiring annual eligibility verification. With a proposed annual funding cap of $4 billion, our policy aims to improve healthcare affordability for financially vulnerable individuals and working families.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$4.00B
AT A GLANCE
  • Up to $2,000 per eligible individual annually
  • Up to two million participants
  • Annual eligibility verification
POLICY 08Social Welfare

Pathways to Financial Independence Act

Providing Support That Creates Long-Term Opportunity

We believe social welfare should guarantee essential support while helping individuals achieve financial independence. Our proposal will offer temporary housing assistance of up to $12,000 annually to 100,000 qualifying households and career-development grants averaging $4,000 for up to 200,000 participants. These programs will prioritize financially vulnerable individuals and working families through income-based eligibility, employment training, and regular progress reviews. With an annual budget of $2.2 billion, we aim to reduce economic barriers while strengthening employment opportunities and long-term self-sufficiency.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$2.20B
AT A GLANCE
  • Temporary housing help for 100,000 households
  • Career-development grants for up to 200,000 people
  • Income-based eligibility and progress reviews
POLICY 09Environment

Corporate Environmental Transparency and Accountability Act

Holding Major Polluters Accountable

We believe that environmental protection is a shared responsibility and that major corporations must be accountable for their environmental impact. Our proposal will strengthen emissions-reporting requirements for major industrial polluters, improve independent verification, and increase transparency through publicly accessible environmental data. An additional $250 million annually will support monitoring, reporting systems, and enforcement against serious violations. These measures will protect public health and natural resources while maintaining clear, consistent environmental standards for businesses.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$250M
AT A GLANCE
  • Industrial emissions disclosure
  • Independent verification
  • Publicly accessible environmental data
POLICY 10Environment

Clean Energy Innovation and Resilience Act

Investing in a Sustainable Economic Future

We propose investing an additional $1.5 billion annually in renewable energy, energy efficiency, and sustainable technological innovation. The program will offer competitive federal grants of up to $4 million each to as many as 300 qualifying projects, encouraging investment in clean electricity, energy storage, and efficient infrastructure. Participating projects will generally be required to secure nonfederal matching contributions and demonstrate measurable environmental and economic benefits. By combining public investment with private-sector innovation, we aim to reduce environmental damage while supporting sustainable economic development.

ADDITIONAL ANNUAL FUNDING$1.50B
AT A GLANCE
  • Grants of up to $4M per project
  • Up to 300 qualifying projects
  • Nonfederal matching contributions generally required

THE FISCAL FRAMEWORK

Ambition with accountability.

$10.36B / YEAR

Our ten policy proposals target approximately $10.36 billion in additional annual federal funding. We propose financing these investments through narrowly targeted tax reforms affecting ultra-high-income earners and preferential tax treatment, rather than broad middle-class tax increases.

Programs will be phased in when necessary, and full implementation will depend on independent revenue and cost estimates. The proposed defense administrative efficiencies are intended for internal reallocation, not treated as guaranteed new revenue.

Budget note: These amounts are party proposals, not enacted federal appropriations or independently scored estimates. The revenue from proposed tax changes is not yet established.

OUR PLATFORM

10 policies. Clear priorities.

POLICY 01Education

Equal Opportunity School Funding Act

Fair funding for every school.

Increase Title I-A funding by 10% for schools in low-income and rural communities. Require three-year improvement plans and public progress reports.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$1.84B
POLICY 02Education

Teacher Excellence and Academic Improvement Act

Better teaching. Better classrooms.

Invest in teacher mentoring, professional training, and proven classroom methods. Keep curriculum decisions local and school results transparent.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$220M
POLICY 03Economy

Fair Competition and AI Accountability Act

Competition should be fair.

Strengthen FTC and DOJ enforcement against monopolies and unfair practices. Set clearer AI training-data rules without overwhelming small businesses.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$150M
POLICY 04Economy

Fair Taxation and Responsible Investment Act

A fairer tax code.

Add a 2% tax on annual income above $5 million for individuals or $10 million for joint filers, reform carried-interest treatment, and strengthen high-income tax enforcement.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$150M
POLICY 05National Defense

Strong Defense, Responsible Engagement Act

Strong defense. Careful decisions.

Add $500 million a year for readiness, cyber defense, and equipment upkeep. Support allies while strengthening congressional oversight of overseas operations.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$500M
POLICY 06National Defense

Constitutional Emergency Powers Protection Act

Security without unchecked power.

Require strict review of emergency limits on civil liberties. Covered restrictions generally expire after 30 days unless lawfully renewed, with access to court review.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$50M
POLICY 07Social Welfare

Working Families Healthcare Affordability Act

Care families can afford.

Offer up to $2,000 a year in extra Marketplace insurance assistance to as many as two million eligible people, focused on households near 138–250% of the federal poverty level.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$4.00B
POLICY 08Social Welfare

Pathways to Financial Independence Act

Support that opens doors.

Provide temporary housing help for up to 100,000 households and career-development grants for up to 200,000 people, with income-based eligibility.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$2.20B
POLICY 09Environment

Corporate Environmental Transparency and Accountability Act

Polluters should answer for their impact.

Require major industrial polluters to report independently verified emissions data. Make the information public and enforce serious violations.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$250M
POLICY 10Environment

Clean Energy Innovation and Resilience Act

Back practical clean technology.

Fund renewable energy, storage, and efficiency projects through competitive grants. Require measurable results and generally seek nonfederal matching funds.

PROPOSED ANNUAL FUNDING$1.50B
